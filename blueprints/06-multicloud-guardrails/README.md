# 06 Multi-Cloud Guardrails

> Status: Planned
> Clouds: AWS and Google Cloud (Terraform)

## In plain terms

**What this protects you from:** AWS and Google Cloud accounts running without the same protections as the rest of your business.

**What it costs to run:** to be published with the first release, with assumptions shown.

## Planned scope

- AWS: account baseline with CloudTrail, GuardDuty, budgets, and root account protection
- Google Cloud: organization policy constraints, audit logging, and budget alerts
- Consistent tagging and labeling conventions across all three clouds

Scope may change as the blueprint is built. Every change and its reasoning will be recorded in the design decisions section of this guide.
